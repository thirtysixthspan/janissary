### notify

Push a custom message into the notifications feed, attributed to the tab that sent it.

**Usage**

`notify <message>`

**Use cases**

- Add a reminder or status message to the session's notifications.
- Make a runscript or another command leave a visible note.

**Examples**

- `notify Build finished` adds `Build finished` to notifications.
- `notify Review the pending changes` adds a reminder to the feed.
