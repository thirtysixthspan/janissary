### profile

Launch a profile, list the available profiles, or validate one.

**Usage**

`profile launch [<name>]`

`profile list`

`profile validate [name]`

**Use cases**

- Reopen an arrangement of tabs and settings from a profile file.
- Check whether a profile is valid.

**Examples**

- `profile launch review` launches the profile named `review`.
- `profile list` opens the list of available profiles.
