### profile

Launch a profile or list the available profiles.

**Usage**

`profile launch [<name>]`

`profile list`

**Use cases**

- Reopen an arrangement of tabs and settings from a profile file.
- Run a profile's runscript, `profiles/<name>.rs`, instead of opening its tabs.

**Examples**

- `profile launch review` launches the profile named `review`.
- `profile list` opens the list of available profiles.
