### notifications

Open or focus the notifications feed, optionally dock it on a side. `notifications clear` empties the queue, saved record, and visible toasts.

**Usage**

`notifications [left|right]`

`notifications clear`

**Use cases**

- Review background activity and diagnostic messages.
- Keep the feed docked while working in another tab.
- Clear queued notifications and toasts.

**Examples**

- `notifications` opens or focuses the notifications feed.
- `notifications left` docks it on the left.
- `notifications clear` clears the notification history.
