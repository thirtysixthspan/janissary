### schedules

Open a combined view of scheduled commands across tabs. Dock the view on either side; its **+** button opens the New schedule dialog.

**Usage**

`schedules`

`schedules left`

`schedules right`

**Use cases**

- Review scheduled commands across the session.
- Keep the schedule list docked while working in another tab.
- Create a schedule from the New schedule dialog.

**Examples**

- `schedules` opens the schedules tab.
- `schedules right` docks the schedules tab on the right.
