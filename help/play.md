### play

Play a file in the tab for its type: recordings open in the recording player, videos in a video tab, and audio files are queued in the audio tab.

**Usage**

`play <path>`

**Use cases**

- Open a recording or video for playback.
- Queue an audio file without selecting its tab type yourself.

**Examples**

- `play devbox` opens the newest `.cast` recording for `devbox`.
- `play clip.mp4` opens the video player for `clip.mp4`.
- `play song.mp3` queues `song.mp3` for audio playback.
