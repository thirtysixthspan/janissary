### nav

Open the fuzzy tab navigator, optionally pre-filled with a search query. Submitting `nav` while the navigator is open closes it.

**Usage**

`nav [query]`

**Use cases**

- Find a tab by typing part of its name.
- Open the navigator already filtered to a query.

**Examples**

- `nav` opens the tab navigator.
- `nav review` opens it with `review` pre-filled.
