### sessions

Open the remote sessions list, showing hosts you are connected to or parked on. The list can be docked on either side.

**Usage**

`sessions`

`sessions left`

`sessions right`

**Use cases**

- Review remote hosts and their connection state.
- Keep the sessions list docked while using another tab.

**Examples**

- `sessions` opens the remote sessions list.
- `sessions left` docks the list on the left.
