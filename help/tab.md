### tab

Focus the first tab whose label or display alias matches the supplied name. The name can contain spaces, and a hidden system tab is revealed when focused.

**Usage**

`tab focus <name>`

**Use cases**

- Switch directly to a tab by its label or display name.
- Reveal and focus a hidden managed system tab.

**Examples**

- `tab focus janus` focuses the tab labelled `janus`.
- `tab focus review notes` searches for the tab named `review notes`.
