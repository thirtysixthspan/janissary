### conversation

Open a saved conversation by title, ignoring case. If no title matches, a new conversation opens and receives the title as its first query.

**Usage**

`conversation <title>`

**Use cases**

- Reopen a saved conversation by its title.
- Start a conversation with an initial question or prompt.

**Examples**

- `conversation Release notes` opens a saved conversation with that title if one exists.
- `conversation Explain the test failures` starts a conversation with that query.
