### conversation

Open a saved conversation by title, ignoring case. If no title matches, a new conversation opens and receives the title as its first query. Bare `conversation` opens the conversation list, or focuses it if it's already open.

**Usage**

`conversation`

`conversation <title>`

**Use cases**

- Reopen a saved conversation by its title.
- Start a conversation with an initial question or prompt.
- Open the list of this project's saved conversations.

**Examples**

- `conversation` opens the conversation list.
- `conversation Release notes` opens a saved conversation with that title if one exists.
- `conversation Explain the test failures` starts a conversation with that query.
- `conversation left` opens or starts a conversation titled `left`. Only `conversations left` docks the list.
