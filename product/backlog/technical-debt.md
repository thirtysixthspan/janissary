Proposal Risk: 2/10 - The terminals' deliberate differences (Ctrl+←/→, Ctrl+R, and Ctrl+E still go to the PTY for word motion, reverse search, and end-of-line) must stay expressed as exclusions beside the shared predicate, and an extraction that folded them in would start stealing those keys from interactive programs.

Proposal: `web/src/useWindowKeys.ts` handles the chords in `handleTabShortcuts` (Shift+Arrow and Cmd+Shift+bracket to `moveTab`) and `ctrlChordOpener` (Ctrl+A task picker, Ctrl+G tab navigator, plus Ctrl+R and Ctrl+E). `harnessKeyFilter` in `web/src/harness/HarnessTab.tsx` re-derives the tab-switch chord and the Ctrl+A/Ctrl+G chords, and `shellKeyFilter` in `web/src/ShellTab.tsx` re-derives the tab-switch chord a second time; `cardKeyFilter` in `web/src/shared/transcript/TerminalCard.tsx` uses a deliberately coarser rule (all Shift/Ctrl keys bubble) and should be left alone. Add a pure module `web/src/shared/terminal/window-chords.ts` exporting `isTabSwitchChord(e)` (Shift+ArrowLeft/Right without Ctrl, Cmd+Shift with `[`, `{`, `]`, `}`) and `isPickerChord(e)` (bare Ctrl+A or Ctrl+G, no Shift/Alt/Meta), with a colocated `window-chords.test.ts`.
