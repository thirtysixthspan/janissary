* embedded-web-page (8/10) — 5 of 59 facts wrong and 1 missing, and every one of them sits in the "Viewing, not driving" framing of `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/web-pages.md`, so the tab's central promise is the part that is wrong. The back and forward buttons are documented as moving the embedded page through its own history; they call `contentWindow.history.back()`, which a cross-origin frame does not expose, so on any real remote page they do nothing — and `product/specs/embedded-web-page.md` asserts the same capability, so the prose cannot simply be copied from the spec. "An address that won't load is discarded without a word" overstates the app: only a malformed address or an unviewable scheme is dropped, while a valid URL to a host that is down, or a 404, is loaded into the frame like any other. The address and label are said to follow in-page navigation "as long as the app is running in its own managed browser", but the relay comes from the bundled browser extension and a managed browser can run without it. The page's own title is said to change on navigation, while the header renders the root domain. The one missing fact is that clicking away from the address field commits the typed address, because the input wires blur to the same commit as Enter. Ground truth is `product/specs/embedded-web-page.md`, `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/web-pages.md`, `web/src/plugins/page/PageTab.tsx`, `web/src/plugins/page/PageAddressInput.tsx`, `src/plugins/page/activate.ts`, `src/openers/web-target.ts`, and `chrome-extension/content-script.js`. * harness (4/10) — 1 of 83 facts missing and none wrong.
