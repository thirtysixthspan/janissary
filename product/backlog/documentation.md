The ground truth is `product/specs/editor-tab.md`, `web/src/editor/`, and `web/src/shared/fuzzy-match.ts`. Fix by extending `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/editor.md`.

The ground truth is `product/specs/harness.md`, `src/harness/`, and `documentation/user-documentation/advanced-agents/harness.md`. Fix by extending that page.
