## development

* tab-navigator (7/10) — 1 of 8 facts missing and 1 wrong, the wrong one being the first thing the page tells you. `documentation/user-documentation/command-bar/tab-navigator.md` says `Ctrl+G` opens "a window listing every open tab — agent, harness, SSH, viewer, and reporting tabs alike", but `web/src/pickers/tab-nav-match.ts` filters its list through `isCenterActionTab`, which drops every docked tab and every reporting tab, so a tree you moved into a sidebar and a monitor's reporting tab are both unreachable from the navigator. `product/specs/tab-navigator.md` carries the same stale claim. The missing fact is the other half of the same sentence: `filterTabs` matches a query against the internal label **or** the displayed name, so a tab you renamed matches on its alias and a view tab matches on its file name, and the highlighted text is that displayed name. The ground truth is `web/src/pickers/tab-nav-match.ts`, `src/tab/placement.ts`, and `product/specs/tab-navigator.md`. Fix by correcting the opening sentence of `documentation/user-documentation/command-bar/tab-navigator.md` and extending its filtering section.

* sessions-tab (4/10) — 5 of 25 facts missing and none wrong. `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/sessions.md` lists the row columns, the states, and the four actions faithfully, but never says what a failed attempt leaves behind: a refused attach or a peer process that no longer exists settles the session for good, turning the row into `terminated`, while a timeout or a failed connection settles nothing — the row stays parked, the **Attach** button stays there to try again, and the trash button appears beside it. Also missing: that every failed attachment records a notification naming the session, the host, and the reason, and that the notification outlives the temporary connection tab; that a peer which accepts an attach but reports no process state is told to shut down and its record dropped rather than holding a remote workspace open; that an agent tab later recreated under the same name starts its own shell instead of binding to a process left by a different session; and that a reporting line is attributed to the sessions tab when it is open and the active tab otherwise. The ground truth is `product/specs/sessions-tab.md` and `src/sessions/`. Fix by extending `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/sessions.md`.

* conversations (4/10) — 4 of 30 facts missing and none wrong. `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/conversations.md` covers the list, the turn view, naming, and the model selector, but never says that the last model pair chosen is remembered across conversations and across restarts, so every new conversation opens on it while sending a query leaves the memory alone. Also missing: that each conversation keeps one live agent session and the next query starts a new one and replays the 20 most recent completed turns before it; that the **Chat about this** selection sits in the turn list as a turn posed by you with nothing sent until you write a prompt, and is not saved, so a tab closed without sending loses it; and that a conversation tab opened out of view does not take focus from the tab you are looking at. The ground truth is `product/specs/conversations.md` and `src/conversations/`. Fix by extending `documentation/user-documentation/tab-types/conversations.md`.
