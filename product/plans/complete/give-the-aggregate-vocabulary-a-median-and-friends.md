# Give the aggregate vocabulary a median, a percentile, a variance and a distinct count

A chart of "how long is a request" drawn with a mean is not coarse, it is wrong: a mean over a long-tailed measure is dragged by the tail into a number that describes no row at all, and nothing on screen disagrees with it. The grammar had five reductions — `sum`, `mean`, `count`, `min`, `max` — where Observable Plot's `group` reducers and Vega-Lite's aggregate operators also carry `median`, `pXX`, `deviation`, `distinct` and `argmin`/`argmax`, so the honest summaries of a distribution were reachable only by the model hand-writing a `derive` per row and hoping it got the arithmetic right.

- `VisualizationAggregate` in `src/protocol/visualizations.ts` and its mirror in `src/plugins/visualizations/shared.ts` gain `median`, `percentile`, `variance` and `distinct`, and the chart shape gains a `percentile` field carrying a whole number from 0 to 100. It is its own field rather than a closed set of `p50`/`p90`/`p95` names because p99 is the number most often wanted, and `optionalShapeOf` in `src/visualizations/chart-spec.ts` holds the two together: a percentile with no number and a number beside an aggregate that is not one are the same mistake in two directions.
- `reduce` in `web/src/plugins/visualizations/chart/aggregate.ts` is now a switch over the whole vocabulary, with each algorithm stated in a comment beside it: median as the sorted middle or the mean of the two middles, percentile by linear interpolation as NumPy and Plot's `pXX` do, sample variance over n-1, and a distinct count over the values a group holds rather than the rows it has. The switch also fixes a latent failure — the old chain of comparisons returned the `min` accumulator for anything it did not recognise, so an aggregate it had never heard of drew `-Infinity` as a bar rather than failing.
- An empty group reduces to `NaN` for everything that answers "how much" and to `0` for the three that answer "how many", because the sum of nothing is zero and a bar of a height nobody measured is not.
- `aggregatePhrase` in `src/visualizations/chart-spec.ts` is a function rather than a table because the percentile's number is the point, and it is now also what the caption under a card uses: the caption and the chart's spoken description had two wordings of the same reduction waiting to drift apart, and the caption's was `${aggregate} of ${y}`, which reads "percentile of latency" and says nothing.
- `AGGREGATE_RULE` in `src/visualizations/reply.ts` tells the model to reach for a median or a percentile on a long-tailed measure, and what the `percentile` field is.

`src/visualizations/chart-spec.test.ts` covers the grammar, the percentile's two refusals, and the sentence each new aggregate leaves behind. `src/visualizations/prompts.test.ts` pins the vocabulary the model is given. `web/src/plugins/visualizations/chart/aggregate.test.ts` pins every algorithm against a group it cannot be confused with, including an even-length median, an interpolated percentile, the worked variance example, an empty group, and a single value's lack of variance. `web/src/plugins/visualizations/VisualizationChartCard.test.tsx` asserts the caption and the description both name the percentile. The spec's list of reductions is rewritten around the distinction the change rests on.
