# Keep the measures the user named when a reply brings twelve of its own

`remembered` in `src/visualizations/metrics.ts` replaced same-named definitions, appended the reply's, and then trimmed the combined list with `.slice(-MAX_METRICS)`. The trim drops the *oldest* entries, and the oldest entries are the user's: a reply defining twelve measures of its own emptied a conversation's named measures in one turn, `metricList` then stopped offering them in the prompt, and every chart naming one was refused from then on with nothing anywhere saying the measure was gone. The bound itself is right — the prompt and the record are rewritten on every turn — but it was spent on the wrong end of the list.

- Room is what the measures already in the record leave, and the ones already there are kept. Of the reply's own, the newest are the ones it is about, so the oldest of them go.
- A definition that *replaces* a measure already there is never what gets trimmed. Without that, preferring the user's measures would turn a correction into a loss: `p95 latency` re-defined as the p99 is the oldest of thirteen definitions in a full conversation, so the correction would be the one dropped. Corrections are separated from the reply's new definitions and take their room first.
- `remembered` returns the names that did not fit, and the agent puts them in the same refusal list a chart that could not be drawn goes in — one list, read by the response, so a measure that went missing is said beneath the model's own words rather than silently. Passing a fresh array into `place` was the first attempt and lost every chart refusal too, because `place` adds to the list it is given; the two now share one.

Two cases in `src/visualizations/metrics.test.ts` fail against the previous trim: twelve incoming definitions over a conversation holding `p95 latency`, which asserts the user's measure survives, the oldest incoming one goes, and the name that went is returned; and a reply correcting that same measure while bringing twelve others, which asserts the correction lands. A case in `agent.test.ts` asserts the refusal reaches the turn.

`place` moved to `src/visualizations/place.ts` while the agent was over the two-hundred-line limit: placing a reply's charts touches the record rather than the exchange, so it needs none of the session, turn or in-flight bookkeeping the agent holds, and it is now a function of the record, the reply's entries, the refusal list and the acquire callback.
