# Add a time unit to the x column and calendar ticks to the axis

A date column was ordered by its instant and then drawn as an ordinal band of its raw values, so a two-year daily series was 730 bands and "revenue by month over two years" was inexpressible — the model's only route was a derived column doing epoch arithmetic it had to get right. Vega-Lite carries a `timeUnit` encoding for exactly this, and a `time` scale with calendar-aware ticks; here the band renderer stays and the calendar enters through the unit and the labels.

- `src/visualizations/time-units.ts` floors an instant to the start of `year`, `quarter`, `month`, `week` or `day`, and `bucketed` rewrites **only the x cells** of a table. The rows are all kept, which is the decision the whole thing rests on: collapsing them here would apply the chart's own aggregate to already-reduced rows, and a mean of per-day means is not the mean the chart would otherwise have drawn. Everything downstream — the transformations, the `limit` that counts distinct categories, the client that groups rows sharing a label — already behaves correctly for a category the source happened to contain.
- A cell that is not a date is left exactly as it was, so a mixed column cannot be half-rewritten into an axis that lies about the rows it kept.
- The floor is in UTC, because a date cell in this feature is a calendar day and not an instant with a zone on it, and a week begins on Monday — a bucket named after a Sunday would be a second calendar on the same axis.
- `resolve` in `src/visualizations/chart-spec.ts` applies the unit *before* the transformations, so a filter on a month and a `limit` counted in months both see the categories the chart is drawn from. `validateChart` refuses a unit on a column that is not a date, naming the unit and the type it found.
- The labels come from `calendarLabel` in the new `web/src/plugins/visualizations/chart/time.ts`, which says a year as a year, a month as a month with the year on the first band, and a quarter as `Aug Q3`. The year on the first month is the load-bearing part: twelve month names with no year on any of them cannot say which year a band is in.
- The caption names the unit (`4 rows · the sum of revenue · by month`) rather than the transformation notes carrying it, because a time unit is not a transformation — nothing was done to the data except to say which day each row belongs to. `chartSummary` says the same in the sentence a chart leaves behind when the model changed it silently.

`src/visualizations/time-units.test.ts` covers the calendar arithmetic itself: a month end, a quarter boundary in each position, the ISO week across a Sunday (the case a Sunday-starting week gets wrong), a year boundary, that bucketing keeps every row, and that a non-date cell is untouched. `src/visualizations/chart-spec.test.ts` covers the grammar, the refusal on a non-date column, the rows surviving bucketing, and a `limit` counting buckets. `web/src/plugins/visualizations/chart/time.test.ts` covers the labels, including the year on January. `VisualizationChartCard.test.tsx` covers the caption. The spec gains a paragraph.
